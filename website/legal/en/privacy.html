Collection and Processing of Data

Contact Form

If you contact us via the contact form on this website, the data you enter (name, email address, message, and optionally phone number and property details) is processed in order to handle your inquiry. Processing is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in processing inquiries).

Your data is used exclusively to respond to your inquiry and is not passed on to third parties without your explicit consent.

Server Log Files

When you access this website, our hosting provider automatically stores information in server log files: IP address, browser type, operating system, date and time of access, and pages accessed. This data is not merged with other data sources and is deleted after 7 days at the latest.

Cookies

This website uses technically necessary cookies required for the operation of the website (e.g. session cookies). Consent is not required for these cookies pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

In addition, we only use analytics cookies (Google Analytics) if you have explicitly consented via our cookie banner. Without your consent, no analytics or marketing cookies are set and no corresponding data is transmitted to third parties.

You may withdraw or grant your consent at any time via the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of this website.

Google Analytics

If you have consented, this website uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google"). Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies that enable an analysis of your use of this website (e.g. pages visited, time spent, origin of access).

Your IP address is shortened by Google within member states of the European Union or in other contracting states of the Agreement on the European Economic Area prior to transmission to the USA (IP anonymisation). The legal basis is your consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

You may withdraw your consent at any time with future effect via the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer. Further information on how Google Analytics handles user data can be found in Google's privacy policy.

Embedded Services

Fonts

This website uses the typefaces Cormorant Garamond, Cinzel and Jost. The font files are served locally from our own server, not from Google or any other third party. No connection to external font servers is made and no data is transmitted to third parties.

Google Maps

On some pages we offer a map view of our office using Google Maps. The map is not loaded automatically. If you selected "Accept All" in our cookie banner, the map is embedded automatically; otherwise it is only loaded once you actively click "Load Google Maps". In both cases, a connection is established to servers of Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA, transmitting your IP address and potentially other usage data to Google. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time via the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer. Further information can be found in Google's privacy policy.

Google Calendar (Appointment Booking)

We use Google Calendar to book initial consultations. When you click the booking link, you are redirected to the Google platform. Google's privacy policy applies.

Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights with respect to personal data concerning you:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)

To exercise your rights, please contact: kontakt@grenford.de

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The competent authority for Berlin is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

Data Security

For security reasons and to protect the transmission of confidential content, this website uses SSL/TLS encryption. You can recognise an encrypted connection by the fact that the browser's address bar changes from "http://" to "https://".
